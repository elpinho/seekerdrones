## What counts as an art asset

Under `src/main/resources/assets/seekerdrones/` and `src/generated/resources/assets/seekerdrones/`:

- `textures/`: all textures, including the GUI sprites.
- `models/`: block, item and entity models.
- `sounds/`: the sounds made for this mod (marked "Synthesized for this mod" in [`SOUND_CREDITS.md`](SOUND_CREDITS.md)).

The scripts in `scripts/textures/` that generate the textures are code (LGPL-3.0). The images they produce are art assets (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).

## Third-party sounds

The other sounds come from Freesound and keep their own licenses (CC0 1.0 or CC BY 4.0). [`SOUND_CREDITS.md`](SOUND_CREDITS.md) lists each file, its author and its license. CC BY 4.0 sounds must keep their credit wherever they're used.

## In short

You may share and adapt the art assets, for example in a fork or a port of the mod, as long as you:

- **credit** Seeker Drones and link to <https://github.com/elpinho/seekerdrones>,
- **don't use them commercially**, and
- **license your changes** to the assets under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 too.

**Modpacks:** as an extra permission on top of the license, you may include the unmodified mod jar, with its assets, in any modpack, including modpacks that earn platform rewards.

This summary isn't a substitute for the license: the legal text linked above is what applies.
